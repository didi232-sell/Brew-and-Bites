About Us

Brew & Bites is a family‑run café in Stockport, designed to feel warm and welcoming with a cosy brown and orange colour scheme that matches the café interior.

We serve freshly brewed coffee, homemade cakes and light bites, and provide a relaxed space for people to meet, study, or unwind.
